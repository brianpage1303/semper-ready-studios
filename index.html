Inside the app

Everything you need, in one calm place

These are real, usable sections — the same tabs and tools as the Android app. Tap any of them to open Warrior Wayfinder™ in this browser. Built first around Upstate South Carolina, with statewide and national organizations and VA events farther afield.

  • Orgs

    SC and national resources for transitioning veterans. Open a card to visit their website. Star an organization to save it.

    Open Orgs in your browser

  • Events

    A monthly local calendar of verified listings, plus VA events, sorted by what’s close when a location or ZIP is set. You can submit an event in the Android app; it stays on your phone as Pending.

    Open Events in your browser

  • Browse

    Short, practical reads on Transition, Jobs, Mental health, Family, Benefits, Housing, Women & families, and stories from vets. Type in the search box to look across orgs, events, and reads.

    Open Browse in your browser

  • Crisis help on every screen

    Call 988, then press 1, or text 838255. A 988 button stays one tap away, and the About screen has the full Veterans Crisis Line card.

    Open crisis help in your browser

  • Your place

    Choose “Use my location” or enter a ZIP or state yourself. Approximate location only, and only when you ask. Works in the 50 states and DC. You can change it any time.

    Set your place in your browser

  • Saved

    Star articles, organizations, and events to keep them on this device. Nothing you save is sent to us.

    Open Saved in your browser

  • Look

    Five color themes, including the default Cream + Navy, plus light and dark mode. Your choice is saved on this device.

    Change the look in your browser

  • Peer Talk

    Coming soon — an interest signup, not live chat. Crisis help stays one tap away. Anything you type is saved on this device only.

    Open Peer Talk in your browser

  • Support a partner

    Donate opens the websites of South Carolina partners such as Semper Fi Barn and Upstate Warrior Solution. Warrior Wayfinder™ never handles your payment.

    Open Support a partner in your browser

How it works

Show what’s near you, then explore

  1. Show what’s near you

    The first time you open the app, choose “Use my location” or “Enter it myself.” We keep only your state and ZIP on this phone. You can change it any time.

  2. Browse Orgs, Events, and short reads

    Use the same tabs as the app — Home, Browse, Orgs, Events, and Saved — to find organizations, a calendar of events, and practical next steps.

  3. Help is one tap away

    If you or a veteran you know is in crisis, tap 988. Call 988, then press 1, or text 838255. Confidential help is available 24/7.

Questions

FAQ

Is Warrior Wayfinder™ free?

Yes. The app is free. There are no accounts, no ads, and no in-app purchases. If you use “Support a partner,” you give on that organization’s own website, not inside Warrior Wayfinder™.

Which phones can download it?

The current app is Android only, through Google Play internal testing. You need an Android phone and the Gmail account on that phone must be on the tester list. Then use the Download for Android button or scan the code.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. An iPhone version is a work in progress and is not available. There is no TestFlight link to share today.

What about my data and privacy?

No accounts, no ads, and no personal data sold. Your location stays on your phone or in this browser. Approximate location is optional; you can type a ZIP or pick a state instead. The app and this website download a public events and organizations list from GitHub. Read the full privacy policy.

How do I join the Android test?

Email brianpage13031582@gmail.com from the Gmail on your Android phone and ask to be added. After Brian adds you, tap Download for Android or scan the QR code on that same phone.

About the maker

A project by Brian Page

Warrior Wayfinder™ is built by Brian Page, a Marine veteran in Seneca, South Carolina, under the name Semper Ready Studios. The mission, from the app itself: a warm anytime info hub for veterans — especially those transitioning to civilian life — so you can find clarity, camaraderie, and next steps without an account or a clinical vibe.
